      DNS resolver
      & monitor

      Rust · networking

      Parses DNS off the wire rather than leaning on a library, caches by TTL, signs answers with an ECDSA key over RRSIG/DNSKEY, serves DNS-over-HTTPS, and drops ad and tracker domains before they leave the network.

      The threat engine scores every query on the Shannon entropy of its subdomain labels, label length, per-IP rate, and how fast one parent domain is growing unique subdomains — the signature of DNS tunneling.

      04

      Book Nook

      Mapbox · OpenStreetMap · scraping · group project

      A book club app built with a team for Software Engineering 2. My two pieces were the map and the Goodreads import.

      The map plots the bookshops, libraries, and cafés a club could meet in. The markers aren't hand-entered — they're pulled out of OpenStreetMap with Overpass, published as Mapbox tilesets, and drawn under the club's own meeting pins. Waco is mapped shop by shop; libraries and cafés are carried nationally.

      The importer scrapes an existing Goodreads account, so a member arrives with their shelves, ratings, and reading history already in the app instead of starting from an empty profile.

      your words What you'd want someone to take away from this one — the map especially, since it's the part you put the time into.

      08
      
          
      Helm
      
          
      TypeScript · React · self-hosted
      
          
      A request-to-playback pipeline runs at home — intake, automated
            organisation, playback around the house — across about sixty containers on one box.
            Sixty containers don't make a picture, so Helm is the picture: a phone app I built to
            watch the stack and act on it.
      
          
      One process holds the Docker socket and nothing else does. It works from a
            fixed set of operations, and anything that could take a service down has to name its
            target — the databases and the things everything else depends on are found automatically
            and need a press-and-hold before they'll go anywhere. Services get added from the phone
            by picking a connector — Sonarr, Radarr, qBittorrent, the request queue, the Minecraft
            servers — and it's reached over Tailscale, so none of it is exposed.
